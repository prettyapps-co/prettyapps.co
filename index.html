Breathe

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Pause Point stays out of the way so you can sit. A few careful features, each one tuned to be quiet.

Turn the ring

Set your session by turning a ring, from a single minute up to six hours. Add an optional warmup countdown to settle in, and optional interval bells to mark the way.

Ambient sounds

Sit with a gentle soundscape, or with silence. Four elemental sounds play softly for as long as your session lasts.

  • Air
  • Fire
  • Earth
  • Water

A singing bowl, not an alarm

A gentle singing-bowl bell marks the end of a session. A notification chimes too, so you'll hear it even if the app is in the background or your screen is off.

Five themes, light and dark

Softly animated gradient backgrounds in five moods. Your Home Screen app icon can match the theme, and every theme has a light and a dark appearance.

  • Amethyst
  • Ember
  • Sol
  • Forest
  • Sky

History that encourages

Every completed session is kept on your device. See your streaks, your totals, and a few gentle insights into how your practice is going.

Mindful Minutes in Apple Health

If you like, Pause Point can log each completed session to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. It only ever writes mindfulness sessions and never reads anything else.

On your wrist, in sync with your phone.

Pause Point comes with a companion Apple Watch app, so you can leave the phone face down and still sit with a timer.

Apple Watch app

Start, follow, and finish a session from your wrist. The Watch app stays in sync with the iPhone app, and the sync happens directly between your own devices.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your session history lives on your device, and Apple Health logging is optional and under your control. Read the Privacy Policy.

Launch offer

Free for early birds.

Pause Point is launching free. No subscription, no account, and nothing to keep track of. Just sit.
